Wind sensor

{% set chosen_surroundings = value(entry, 'wind_surroundings') %}

Forecasts and weather models give wind at 10 m above open ground, where it blows harder than lower down. With the anemometer's height above the ground (a roof's height included) and what surrounds it, wind is also stored as an estimate at 10 m, which the dashboards show first. {% if item.wind_factor != 1 %}Saved, these give ×{{ "%.2f"|format(item.wind_factor) }}.{% endif %} A change applies to readings from then on; earlier ones keep the factor they were stored with. While the cups are too slow to turn, below 1 m/s, the estimate is the weather model's wind.
